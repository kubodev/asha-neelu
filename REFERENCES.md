## Interpretation

The map labels alternate between MDR33 in Malkapuram and Vijayawada–Amaravathi Road in Amaravati at nearby viewpoints. The inset road geometry ties the scene to the reservoir neighbourhood. Labels identify places on the map, but do not establish survey points or building ownership.

The July 2022 photograph provides historical material and road-width context. Later views show rough exposed ground near the junction, and their road surfaces should be read in their own dates and viewpoints. None clearly shows a close, unobstructed water surface. Water colour in the baseline follows the overhead evidence. The visible green patch does not establish its cause or measured water quality.

The supplied ground images do not identify household service connections, the reported well, pipe routes, or measured elevations. Village-level field reports therefore have no household or well pin.

## Adding or replacing references

Keep originals in the workspace img folder. Add a display JPEG and a thumbnail under dist/assets/photos or dist/assets/references. Add metadata in dist/photos.js. Keep the stable ref ID when replacing a display conversion of the same source. Use a new ID for a different capture. A photo location is the local [x,z] pair, with x increasing eastward and z southward. Record the source, capture date, heading and uncertainty. Leave missing values explicit.
